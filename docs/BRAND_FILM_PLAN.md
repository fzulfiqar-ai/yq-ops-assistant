## A. Positioning Statement

YQ Bahrain turns any counter with foot traffic — hypermarket checkout, pharmacy, salon, car wash, cold store, mobile shop — into an accessories profit line, backed by an exclusive VFAN range, one consolidated purchase order, and a named local operator who restocks before Bahrain wakes up. For wholesalers: proven sell-through, full-category coverage, and untapped channel whitespace under one partner.

**Audience (wholesale-first, all channels):** 1) wholesalers/sub-distributors · 2) modern-trade buyers (supermarkets/hypermarkets) · 3) mobile shops · 4) non-traditional footfall venues (pharmacies, salons, car washes, cold stores, petrol kiosks). Consumers are the secondary audience — their visible desire is the retailer's evidence.

## B. Core Creative Concept — "COUNTED ON"

One day of the founder's rounds, filmed like a doctor doing morning rounds. He opens the warehouse at Fajr light, loads the van, and moves counter to counter across the island. At every stop the same ritual repeats — shelf filled, WhatsApp thumbs-up, product moving — until the viewer understands the thesis without being told it:

> **Every counter in Bahrain is an accessories counter — and YQ Bahrain is the partner behind it.**

Double meaning of the title: counters are *counted on*; he is who Bahrain's trade *counts on*. We never sell cables — we film the ritual of supply. Velocity is SHOWN, never claimed: the same cold-store clip strip full at dawn and half-empty at dusk; a reorder message — "Same again Tuesday 👍". Emotional register: calm operator confidence. Four voiceover sentences in the whole film — it is confident enough to shut up.

Selected via a 4-concept competition judged on B2B conversion, AI-production feasibility, and craft; winner with 2 of 3 judges. Seven elements grafted from the losing concepts: the deal-gesture close, the credential super, the dead-phone-save macro, the FOMO line, the two-texture grade, diegetic retail percussion, and the 2-second map-of-dots scale beat.

**Tagline (recommended):** *Every counter earns. / كل كاونتر يربح*
Alternates: *Stocked before Bahrain wakes.* · *One partner. Every counter.*
**Credential super (all end cards):** *Exclusive VFAN Distributor — Bahrain*
**FOMO line (20s cut / deck):** *Your customers already want these. The question is whose counter they'll find them on.*

## G. Keeper Shot Strategy

Target **~15 final keeper AI clips** (S06/S17 double-budgeted). Discipline: 3 still variants per shot → contact sheet → owner approves ONE → animate only the approved still. All founder shots generated in one batch session with the frozen wardrobe block; silhouette/three-quarter-back for the riskiest founder shots (S01, S04); only one true close-up (S15a). Negative prompt ported from `app/agnes.py` ("warped product, deformed, text artifacts, watermark, logo, morphing, flickering…"). Every generation logged in `generations.csv`.

## H. Production Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI hands (S06, S17) | 2× variants; designed no-hands fallbacks (strip-already-full / box-already-placed push-ins) |
| Soul ID face drift | Enlarged photo set in `footage/me/` (owner adding 5–10 photos); founder medium-distance by default; one close-up only |
| AI fake shelf/pack text | Shallow DOF, angled counters, generic blurred packs in prompts; paint-out in post; real art comped on flats |
| Product fidelity | Never ask AI to render recognizable VFAN packaging; real 5000px carton art composited on locked/slow shots only |
| Empty-Bahrain feel (one-human rule) | Hold the "before Bahrain wakes" framing consistently — dawn light, clocks, time supers |
| WhatsApp UI legal | Stylized recreation, purple-tinted, no Meta trade dress |
| Claims discipline | Velocity shown (half-empty strip, reorder message), never stated; no "best/#1/cheapest" anywhere incl. supers |
| Arabic quality | Gulf-dialect review by owner; IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Kufi Arabic via libass; correct RTL |
| Music licensing | Buy-out/CC0 track into `assets/music/` (wired hook in `video_gen.py`); Status videos get re-forwarded |
| YQ logo 512px ceiling | Card lockup rebuilt as typeset Space Grotesk + icon composite; clean upscale only if needed |

## I. Post-Production Plan

Local FFmpeg 8.1.2 full build (NVENC GPU encode). Assembly adapts the proven `assemble_reel()` grammar in `app/video_gen.py`: plates → uniform encode params → concat-demuxer. Branding composited from REAL files in `assets/brand/` (`yq-icon-512.png`, `yq-logo-transparent.png`, `vfan-logo.png`) — never typeset-only, never AI-generated. Screen replacements + UI animations rendered as Pillow frame sequences → FFmpeg overlay (static cameras make tracking trivial). Two-texture grade via FFmpeg LUTs: atmosphere clips glossy, proof clips grittier-neutral. Supers: Space Grotesk + Inter + Arabic companion via libass/drawtext. Sound: WhatsApp pings motif, diegetic retail percussion, 90-BPM track, the silence beat, cha-ching button; VO via edge-tts or recorded by owner.
